Our Story

Cooked the slow way, on purpose

Saffron & Smoke started as a small charcoal grill with one family recipe. Today we still marinate overnight, grind our spices in-house and cook over real wood.

No shortcuts. Just good ingredients, patient cooking and a table full of people you love.
